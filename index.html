I am currently an M.S. student in the Department of Technology Management at Korea University and a member of the Financial Technology Laboratory. My current research focuses on constructing asset covariance matrices using factor models and applying them to portfolio optimization. I am particularly interested in factor modeling, covariance estimation, and the integration of factor-based approaches with portfolio construction. More broadly, I am interested in quantitative finance and data-driven methods for investment and risk management.
